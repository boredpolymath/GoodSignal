# Code of Conduct

Good Signal welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Everyone who participates in project spaces is expected to help keep them respectful and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and open to differing viewpoints.
- Give and receive constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus discussion on the work and its impact.
- Respect people's privacy. Do not post personal information, private device data, or security reports publicly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, insults, threats, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and other conduct that would make project spaces unsafe or hostile are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in Good Signal repositories, issues, pull requests, and other spaces when someone is representing the project. Project maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to address unacceptable behavior.

Report a conduct concern privately to the project maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/boredpolymath). Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. The maintainer will review reports and handle them as confidentially as practical.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
