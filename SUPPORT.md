# Support

Good Signal is an informational checklist app, not a device diagnostic service. It does not inspect or control devices, and guide coverage is generic unless a guide explicitly says otherwise.

## Get help or report an app issue

- Read the [README](README.md) for what the app does, how local data works, and its limits.
- For an app bug or feature request, [open an issue](https://github.com/boredpolymath/GoodSignal/issues/new/choose) using the matching template. Include your browser and operating system where relevant, but do not include account credentials, personal information, or private device data.
- For a security concern, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.

## Report a guide correction

Use **Report an outdated or incorrect guide** from the relevant guide page. Good Signal prepares a report locally for you to download or share; it does not transmit the report automatically. To get it to the maintainer, open a [guide report issue](https://github.com/boredpolymath/GoodSignal/issues/new?template=guide-correction.yml) and include the report text or relevant details. Remove any personal or sensitive information first.

Guide reports are reviewed under the process in [Guide Coverage and Maintenance](docs/Guide-Coverage-and-Maintenance.md). A submitted correction does not imply that the guide or a particular device has been verified.
